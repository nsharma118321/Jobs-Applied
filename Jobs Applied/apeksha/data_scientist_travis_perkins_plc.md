## Job description captured at application time

BSS and TF Solutions, part of Travis Perkins plc, are now hiring for an exceptional Data Scientist to join our Commercial team and play a pivotal role in transforming our pricing intelligence and business performance.

You'll be a trusted technical partner to Commercial, Sales and Branch teams, converting complex datasets into intuitive tools, clear visualisations and actionable strategies that directly protect and grow our margins.

If you are a commercially minded, data driven professional who excels at hands-on coding, statistical modelling and leveraging cutting-edge AI tools like Gemini to solve commercial challenges, we'd love to hear from you!

The Opportunity

As our Data Scientist, reporting directly to the Head of Pricing (BSS & TF Solutions), you will support the core pricing function by using data science, statistical modelling and reporting to sharpen commercial decision-making.

Working in a key individual contributor role, you will analyse large complex transaction datasets, build robust pricing guidance models and create user friendly dashboards for non-technical commercial stakeholders. You will also champion modern analytical practices, utilising coding tools to accelerate testing, automation and iterative development within robust data governance standards.

What You'll Be Doing

- Pricing Analytics & Modelling: Developing, maintaining and enhancing predictive models that power price guidance, margin performance, supplier support analysis, and cost to price pass through metrics. 
- Price Guidance & Quantile Analysis: Analysing transaction data, customer behaviour and product tiers to refine quantile based pricing structures and optimise price execution. 
- Statistical & Regression Analysis: Applying statistical methods and regression techniques to model commercial relationships, uncover margin trends and evaluate pricing strategies. 
- Dashboard & Visualisation Development: Building clear, interactive and automated analytical outputs for commercial stakeholders using tools such as Looker Studio, Google Sheets, Power BI, or Tableau. 
- AI-Assisted Coding & Automation: Leveraging AI tools (preferably Gemini) to streamline coding workflows, automate testing, maintain documentation and speed up analytical iterations. 
- Commercial Insights & Recommendations: Translating complex mathematical and statistical findings into practical, commercially grounded advice for Branch, Sales and Finance teams. 
- Data Quality & Governance: Validating, reconciling and auditing pricing data across core systems to safeguard data integrity and ensure highly dependable commercial decisions. 
- Supplier Support & Rebate Analysis: Analysing cost changes, rebate structures, and vendor support data to drive superior gross margin outcomes. 

About You

You'll be a curious, careful, and collaborative analytical practitioner who balances statistical rigour with fast paced commercial delivery.

Essential

- Degree level qualification in Data, Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Economics, Operational Research, Engineering, or a similar quantitative discipline. 
- Strong SQL proficiency and practical Python coding capabilities. 
- Proven capability in using AI tools (Gemini preferred) to speed up code development, debugging and testing. 
- Demonstrated experience in regression analysis, quantile analysis and managing complex structured datasets. 
- Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with an ability to translate technical concepts for non-technical teams. 
- Advanced spreadsheet capability alongside an understanding of data quality, validation and governance standards. 
- Self starter mindset with the ability to manage tasks independently, work through ambiguity and comfortably challenge assumptions when the data does not support them. 

We'd Particularly Like to See

- Master's degree or equivalent postgraduate qualification in a quantitative discipline. 
- Prior experience within a pricing, finance, commercial, merchanting, retail, or B2B distribution environment. 
- Hands-on experience developing dashboards in Looker Studio, Power BI, or Tableau, alongside experience with GCP, BigQuery, or automated data pipelines. 
- Knowledge of machine learning techniques, clustering algorithms, LFL metrics and rebate/margin bridge analysis. 

Why Join Us?

This is a highly visible technical role with direct impact across BSS and TF Solutions. You'll have the opportunity to:

- Shape and modernise commercial decision making across two leading industry brands. 
- Partner directly with Commercial Directors, Pricing Heads and operational leadership. 
- See the direct financial result of your mathematical and analytical models on group performance. 
- Develop your career within BSS and the wider Travis Perkins Group network.
