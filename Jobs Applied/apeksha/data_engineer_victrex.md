## Job description captured at application time

At Victrex, we're transforming the way we use data to drive innovation, operational excellence and business performance across our global organisation. We're looking for a talented Data Engineer to join our growing IT team and play a pivotal role in designing, building and optimising the data platforms that power decision-making across the business.

This is an exciting opportunity for someone who enjoys solving complex data challenges, working with modern cloud technologies and collaborating with stakeholders to turn business requirements into scalable data solutions.

What You'll Be Doing

Responsibilities

As a Data Engineer, you'll be responsible for developing and maintaining robust, high-performing data solutions that support analytics, reporting and AI-driven insights. Working closely with Business Analysts, Architects, Power BI Developers and business stakeholders, you'll:

- Design and develop data pipelines using Microsoft Fabric, Azure and modern data engineering tools.
- Manage data throughout its lifecycle, from ingestion through to consumption, using a medallion architecture approach.
- Build and optimise data models, transformations and integrations using SQL, PySpark and related technologies.
- Monitor, maintain and improve the performance, reliability and scalability of data platforms.
- Support CI/CD deployment practices, source control and code versioning.
- Identify and resolve data quality issues, ensuring trusted and accurate business information.
- Contribute to the ongoing evolution of Victrex's enterprise data and analytics capabilities.

What We're Looking For

We're interested in hearing from individuals who combine strong technical expertise with a collaborative, solution-focused mindset.

You'll Ideally Have Experience With

- Microsoft Fabric and Azure data services
- Data warehousing and modern cloud data platforms
- SQL Server, Azure SQL and advanced T-SQL development
- Data modelling and ETL/ELT processes
- Power BI and analytics environments
- Python, PySpark, SparkSQL or DBT
- CI/CD and source control practices
- Agile delivery methodologies

Experience working within manufacturing, supply chain or ERP-driven environments would be advantageous.

Why Join Victrex?

At Victrex, you'll be part of a global organisation that values innovation, collaboration and continuous improvement. You'll have the opportunity to work with modern technologies, contribute to strategic data initiatives and help shape the future of data and analytics across the business.

We offer a competitive reward package, flexible working arrangements and the opportunity to develop your career within a market-leading organisation at the forefront of high-performance materials.

Ready to help us unlock the value of data? We'd love to hear from you.

About Victrex

Victrex is an innovative world leader in high-performance PEEK (Poly Ether Ether Ketone) and PAEK (Poly Aryl Ether Ketone) polymer solutions focusing on the strategic markets of automotive, aerospace, energy and industrial, electronics and medical. Every day, millions of people use products and applications containing our materials – from smartphones, aeroplanes and cars to energy operations and medical devices.

With over 40 years‘ experience, we are developing world leading solutions with PEEK and PAEK-based polymers and selected semi-finished and finished parts which enable environmental and societal benefit for our customers and our markets and drive value for our shareholders
