The Role: Data Analyst

Transform is looking for a Data Analyst to sit within the data analytics team.

Key Responsibilities:
- Performing ad-hoc data extractions using SQL to build analysis tables to answer client queries
- Support other areas of the analytics team with digital analytics queries using Google Analytics and Tag Manager to set up tracking
- Support BI and reporting deliverables where necessary

Key Skills/Experiences:
- At least 1.5 years hands-on Analytics experience
- Previous industry experience analysing a wide range of data sources
- Ability to work independently and work on multiple client accounts, meeting tight deadlines
- Excellent business problem-solving skills with evidence of developing creative analytical solutions to clients’ problems and telling a convincing story with data
- Ability to evaluate and query large unstructured datasets and provide meaningful insight and reporting metrics
- The ability to communicate and present findings to both technical and non-technical parties
- Able to work collaboratively within the analytics team, supporting other team members with QA of code and analysis outputs
- An understanding of how analytical outputs can be used to drive value in a commercial situation
- Good interpersonal skills
- Structured and methodical working practices and ability to pick things up quickly
- A growth mindset
- AI curious and ready to embrace opportunities
