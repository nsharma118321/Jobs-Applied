## Job description captured at application time

Data Engineering Consultant
Permanent | UK Wide | Hybrid Working (2 days a week in-office)

We're partnering with a highly respected, award-winning technology consultancy that's continuing to invest heavily in its Data & AI practice. As demand continues to grow, they're looking for experienced Data Engineering Consultants to help deliver large-scale cloud data transformation projects for some of the UK's most recognisable organisations.

What You'll Be Doing:

As a Data Engineering Consultant, you'll work closely with clients to understand their business challenges before designing and delivering scalable, cloud-native data solutions.

Your responsibilities will include:
- Designing and developing modern data platforms using Databricks and cloud technologies.
- Building robust, scalable data pipelines using PySpark and Python.
- Creating efficient ELT/ETL processes and implementing best practices around data quality and governance.
- Optimising data models and data architectures for performance and scalability.
- Collaborating with Solution Architects, Data Scientists, Analysts and wider engineering teams.
- Leading technical workstreams and contributing to successful end-to-end project delivery.
- Providing technical guidance and mentoring to junior consultants where appropriate.
- Keeping up to date with emerging technologies and helping clients adopt modern data engineering practices.

What We're Looking For:

We're looking for experienced consultants who combine strong technical skills with excellent communication and stakeholder management.

You'll have:
- Strong commercial experience delivering modern Data Engineering solutions.
- Experience with Databricks and PySpark.
- Strong programming skills in Python and SQL.
- Experience building and optimising cloud-based data pipelines.
- Strong understanding of data modelling, data warehousing and modern data architecture principles.
- Excellent communication skills and the confidence to engage directly with clients.
- A consultative mindset with the ability to solve complex business problems through technology.

The company are also scaling in other modern Data Platform Technologies, so they would also be interested in candidates coming from a Snowflake or Microsoft Fabric background.

Benefits:
- Competitive base salary - Up to £80k base depending on experience.
- Performance-related bonus.
- Clear career progression and opportunities to move into Technical Lead or Solution Architect positions.
- Continuous learning, certifications and professional development.
- Private medical insurance.
- Employer pension contribution.
- Life assurance.
- 25 days annual leave plus bank holidays - With a further buy/sell scheme.
- Access to online learning platforms and funded professional certifications.
