Data Scientist
London or Leicester (2 days per week)

Join a growing Data Science team where you'll build machine learning solutions that directly influence customer engagement, retention, and commercial performance. - Develop anomaly detection solutions to identify issues impacting customer journeys and performance
- Design and evaluate experiments, including A/B testing
- Partner with Product, Marketing, Finance, CRM, and Commercial teams to solve business problems
- Translate complex analysis into actionable recommendations for stakeholders
Your Skills & Experience
- Strong commercial Data Science experience in customer, marketing, ecommerce, loyalty, or subscription environments
- Advanced Python and SQL skills
- Experience building predictive models and deploying data-driven solutions
- Strong understanding of experimentation, statistics, and machine learning
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills
- Experience with Databricks and ML lifecycle tools is beneficial
