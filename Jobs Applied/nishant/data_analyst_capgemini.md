## Job description captured at application time

About the Job you are considering:

Business Data Analyst is responsible for collecting, analyzing, and interpreting business data to help organizations make informed decisions, improve operational efficiency, and achieve strategic objectives. They work closely with business stakeholders, IT teams, and management to identify trends, develop insights, and translate data into actionable recommendations.

Hybrid working:

The places that you work from day to day will vary according to your role, your needs, and those of the business; it will be a blend of Company offices, client sites, and your home; noting that you will be unable to work at home 100% of the time.

Your Role:

- Gather analyze and document business and data requirements through workshops and stakeholder interviews.
- Perform data profiling data discovery and impact analysis across multiple source systems.
- Analyze sourcetotarget mappings business rules transformations and reconciliation requirements.
- Identify Critical Data Elements CDEs and define business attributes data ownership and usage requirements.
- Translate business requirements into user stories functional specifications and process flows.
- Data Lineage Management.
- Document endtoend business and technical data lineage from source systems to reporting and consumption layers.
- Map data flows transformations dependencies and controls across upstream and downstream systems.
- Identify lineage gaps inconsistencies and risks and coordinate remediation activities.
- Support lineage tooling and metadata management platforms such as Microsoft Purview Collibra Alation or Informatica Data Governance.

Your Skills:

- Support implementation of Data Governance frameworks policies standards and operating models.
- Develop and maintain business glossaries data dictionaries metadata repositories and governance artefacts.
- Partner with Data Owners and Data Stewards to define ownership accountability and governance controls.
- Facilitate governance forums workshops and data stewardship activities.
- Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and internal governance standards.
- Data Quality Controls.
- Define data quality rules monitoring metrics and control frameworks.
- Perform rootcause analysis of data issues and work with stakeholders on remediation plans.
- Support data quality assessments reconciliations and control testing.
- Track and report governance KPIs data quality metrics and issue resolution progress
