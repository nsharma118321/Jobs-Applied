## Job description captured at application time

The Data Scientist provides in-house expertise in forecasting, scenario planning, and advanced modelling to support evidence-based decision-making across VisitScotland. The role develops models to inform strategic decisions on visitor dispersal, spend, sustainability, and satisfaction, working closely with colleagues across Data & Insight to ensure robust data inputs and accessible outputs. The postholder translates complex analytical findings into clear insights for stakeholders and supports the evaluation of economic impact, policy, and tourism performance.
