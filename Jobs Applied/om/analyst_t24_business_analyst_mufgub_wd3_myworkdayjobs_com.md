About the Role Position Title: T24 Business Analyst SME Corporate Title: Analyst Internal Title:Analyst Reporting to: AVP/ VP Location: Bangalore Purpose of the Role: To play a key role in enhancement of Core Banking System (CBS) and Downstream Reporting from business perspective Main Responsibilities: Evaluate business processes and reporting requirement, document the requirement and co-ordinate with the development team Lead the discussion of gap analysis including documentation like creation of BRD, FSD , BRFSD etc Trouble shooting BAU system issues, recording and tracking in JIRA /Confluence tools - liaison with HO and Technical teams for timely closure Ensure solid partnerships across branches and maintain relationships with project stakeholders Collaborate and co-ordinate with the Technical BA and global partners including vendor Develop and execute User Acceptance Test and Migration Plans Work closely with Operations team, CB team and vendors and ensure the timelines are met without any delays/issues. Understand the current process/Product flow and bring solutions for manual process steps, either via Automation via CB or other modes which can be tied up to the process overall. Identify issues /risks in terms of current process flow and bring right mitigant to protect banks overall Risk parameters. Conduct meetings and presentations to share ideas and findings. Collaborate with stakeholders ensuring solutions and prioritizing initiatives to meet business needs and requirements. Monitoring deliverables and ensuring timely completion of projects. Translate business requirements into technical specifications for T24 system configuration and customization. Work closely with the development and technical teams to ensure successful system implementations and integrations. Provide expertise in T24 modules, configurations, and functionality to support project requirements. Maintain project related data repository and prepare business process documentation. Provide walkthrough and training to business users as required. Expertise / knowledge required 2 years of relevant working experience in bank/GCC in Operations - Payments, Trade finance, Deposits, Loans, Treasury, Accounting and managing functional business analyst teams. Experience of business requirement creation, analyzing and publishing the requirements as per requirements. Basic understanding of CBS products and Regulatory and Internal Reporting (Loans, Treasury, Deposits, Funds Transfer, Export/ Import, Accounting/ Information, Common Function) Strong organizational, oral, and written communication skills required Working knowledge on project tools like JIRA, Confluence, MS Project, Visio etc End to end experience of CBS implementation project, especially T24 – AA/TPH/MD/LD modules and T24 Analytics is preferred Experience of managing end to end projects including migration from legacy to T24 . Requirements: University graduate in business or related field Exceptional analytical and conceptual thinking skills. The ability to influence stakeholders and work closely with them to determine acceptable solutions. Advanced technical, documentation, fundamental analytical and conceptual thinking skills.
