Job Title Principal Business Solution Analyst, AI Delivery Hub About the Role As a Functional Lead Analyst within the AI Delivery Hub, you will serve as a key link between Product Managers and engineering teams, helping translate visionary concepts into functional, scalable, and user-focused solutions. You will work at the intersection of travel technology and artificial intelligence, supporting the creation of innovative tools that connect Amadeus travel capabilities—such as flight booking, hotel content, check-in, and travel-document verification—to leading AI platforms. In this position, you’ll collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders, clarify business needs, document requirements, and shape solutions ready for production in a dynamic, exploratory environment. In this role, you’ll: Collaborate with Product Managers, engineers, architects, QA teams, and AI-assistant partners to transform product vision into actionable and scalable requirements. Analyze business and functional needs, and translate them into clear, well-documented user stories and technical recommendations. Facilitate functional workshops, ensuring understanding across finance, legal, security, and operations teams. Partner with engineers during the design and build phases, providing guidance and ensuring clarity of functionality and requirements. Document and communicate progress, sharing updates and validated solutions at key milestones. Conduct cross-functional analysis across internal systems and external providers to support design and integration. Contribute to architecture planning, offering functional insights for new product concepts. Simplify complex technical topics, presenting them accessibly to diverse audiences, including cross-functional teams and AI-partner stakeholders. Support testing and validation activities, ensuring traceability and system integrity across product experiences. Mentor colleagues and foster collaboration, helping new members integrate and succeed. What Success Looks Like Clear and consistent translation of product vision into actionable requirements. Strong collaboration and mutual trust between product, engineering, and partner teams. Teams deliver reliable, high-quality solutions efficiently. Documentation remains current, complete, and accessible. Knowledge sharing and mentorship are a core part of team culture. Your Profile Experience bridging business and technology, preferably across complex or cross-functional projects. Ability to perform functional and technical analysis, simplifying complexity into clarity. Strong skills in communication, coordination, and documentation across diverse audiences. Comfortable working in dynamic, innovative, and evolving environments. Demonstrated ability to mentor, coach, and promote collaborative problem-solving across teams.
