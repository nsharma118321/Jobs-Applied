What You’ll Take On (The Actual Job Description)

So you’re looking for a new challenge? This is your chance to step into a role with real responsibility. You’ll be given the space to own your work, supported to move as fast as you want and expected to keep raising the bar for yourself and the business.

AI Engineer – Behavioural & Decision Systems. This is a hands‑on engineering role for someone who wants their models to matter. You’ll take advanced behavioural and decision‑based AI and turn it into production systems that sit at the core of live digital products. Your work will shape how our platforms predict behaviour, make decisions and adapt in real time, powering Digital Twins, agentic systems and intelligent automation across the business. You’ll operate at the point where modelling meets delivery, with real ownership from data through to live impact.

What you’ll do
- Turn behavioural and sequential models into production‑grade systems that directly influence real decisions and outcomes.
- Own end‑to‑end AI pipelines, from raw data to monitored, reliable inference in live products.
- Embed causal thinking into how we evaluate impact, helping teams understand what actually changes behaviour and why.
- Build AI services that integrate cleanly into existing platforms, shaping APIs, events and decision flows alongside engineers.
- Bring behavioural dynamics to life in Digital Twins and agentic systems, making complex decision processes observable and actionable.

This role is built for someone who backs themselves, takes ownership and wants to see their work used not parked in notebooks. If you’re motivated by pace, accountability and the chance to build systems that genuinely change how products behave, you’ll feel at home here.
