Invest in and enhance model development, testing, and release pipelines for efficient and reliable deployment Work closely with product managers, engineering teams, and senior leadership to define use cases, set priorities, and bring production-grade AI systems to life Run A/B tests, iterate based on performance feedback, and ensure alignment with product experience goals and creative quality standards Stay ahead of the curve in the fast-moving GenAI space; guide strategy, share insights internally, and represent the team in technical forums as needed Skills and attributes for success Proven experience with deep learning architectures for generative tasks (e.g., diffusion models, transformers, GANs) Strong understanding of LLMs, with hands-on experience in fine-tuning, prompt engineering, and evaluating foundation models for applied use-cases for multimodal generation Preferred understanding of computer vision, natural language processing, and multimodal learning Strong exposure to ML evaluation techniques, including perceptual quality metrics and user-in-the-loop testing Proficiency in programming languages such as Python, R, and SQL Experience with big data technologies (e.g., Hadoop, Spark) and cloud platforms (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud) Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to present complex information to non-technical stakeholders.
