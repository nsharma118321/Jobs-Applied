ABOUT THE TEAM

Data Analytics is a highly visible function that directly shapes Checkout.com's success. You'll join the Business & Operations Analytics squad, working alongside product managers, designers, and software and analytics engineers to influence the strategy of our Operations and Compliance products.

You'll be backed by Senior Data Scientists, Analytics Engineers, and Data Product Managers as you grow your practice.

THE ROLE

You'll work across two connected domains:

 - AML Transaction Monitoring — the automated rules that flag suspicious transactions for review. You'll improve how those rules work and help automate alert handling.

 - Merchant Care — the tools and support experience for our merchants. You'll measure whether new tools save time, and mine support conversations to show product teams what to build next.

You'll define how we measure product success, shape what data we collect, build the frameworks that turn it into insight, and surface opportunities to improve the product.

WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Measure impact — build frameworks for the efficiency and ROI of new tooling in Merchant Care, so we know which changes are worth it.

 - Protect data quality — partner with engineers to capture and model the right data for trustworthy insights.

 - Optimise AML rules — run simulations on past data to see how rule changes affect review backlogs, and recommend better logic for our SQL-based alerts.

 - Automate alerts — help build and test an agent that clears clearly low-risk alerts, freeing specialists for the cases that matter.

 - Find insight in text — cluster and summarise support conversations (using AI) to spot recurring merchant problems and guide the roadmap.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE

In your first 6–12 months, you'll help:

 - deflect % of Merchant Care contacts through better tooling;

 - deliver rule-threshold recommendations that cut review backlogs for Compliance and Product;

 - get the automated alert-handling agent tested and into use.

WHAT WE'RE LOOKING FOR

 - SQL (essential) — advanced data interrogation. This is the core technical requirement and is assessed in the process.

 - Python or R (a plus) — scripting for data science strengthens your application, but doesn't substitute for strong SQL.

 - Data science foundations — statistics, clustering, and basic NLP. No production ML needed, but you know how to apply these methods.

 - Analytical mindset — you turn messy operational problems into structured, data-informed solutions.

 - Communication — you explain insights and analytical logic clearly to non-technical stakeholders.

 - Experience — ~2–3 years in professional data science or product analytics, with at least 1–2 in a fast-moving startup/scale-up. A strong internship can count towards this alongside 1–2 years of professional work; this is not an entry-level role, and internships alone won't be enough.

NICE TO HAVE

 - Exposure to product analytics, or risk / compliance / financial-crime data.

 - A high-growth technology or payments background.
