Role Context About the organisation We support peace and prosperity by building connections, understanding and trust between people in the UK and countries worldwide. We uniquely combine the UK's deep expertise in arts and culture, education and the English language, our global presence and relationships in over 100 countries, our unparalleled access to young people, creatives and educators, and our own creative sparkle. About the role The role sits as part of the Data Evidence and Performance (DEP) team is part of the Chief Operating Officer function which includes the central oversight and leadership for corporate planning, performance management, evaluation and reporting, the management of strategic risk and of investment, internal audit, shared services, marketing, digital and technology. The British Council is committed to becoming a more data-driven and performance-led organisation, with Strategy 2030 emphasising stronger accountability, better use of data and clearer demonstration of impact. The organisation operates a Hub and Spoke model for Data and Analytics: The central Hub provides enterprise standards, tools, governance, advanced capability and corporate insight Spokes embedded in business areas focus on operational and domain-specific analytics This role sits in the central Hub and has a cross-organisational remit, working closely with: English and Exams and Cultural Engagement Finance, Procurement, HR and Digital & Technology BI Analysts embedded in Spokes The role operates in a complex global organisation with multiple data sources, varying maturity and high external accountability Role Purpose The Business Intelligence (BI) Analyst (corporate performance) plays a key role in enabling data-driven decision-making across the organisation. The role is responsible for delivering high-quality performance analysis, reporting and insight that supports enterprise decision-making, accountability and delivery of Strategy 2030. Working within the central Data Evidence and Performance (DEP) Hub, the role leads on corporate-level performance reporting, including KPIs, dashboards, management information (MI) and analysis for the Senior Leadership Team, Trustees and FCDO. The role ensures that performance insight is timely, accurate, consistent and trusted, drawing on enterprise data standards and governance. The role plays a key part in embedding a performance-led culture, translating complex organisational data into clear, actionable insight that enables leaders to understand progress, risks and opportunities. Main Accountabilities Create and maintain performance reporting frameworks, ensuring alignment with strategic goals and KPIs. Collect, prepare, and manage data from multiple sources, ensuring accuracy and fitness for purpose. Build and maintain interactive dashboards and reports using tools such as Power BI or Tableau. Translate complex datasets into clear narratives and actionable recommendations for non-technical audiences. Support teams in identifying key business questions and defining the data needed to address them. Conduct deep-dive analysis to evaluate marketing campaigns, pricing strategies, and customer behaviour. Ensure compliance with data governance, ethics, and privacy policies. Collaborate with other data professionals across the organisation to improve data quality, accessibility, and integration. Build stakeholder understanding of analytics outputs through training and guidance. Contribute to the development of the British Council's data community by sharing best practices and learning Requirements of the role Minimum/essential Degree, preferably in a relevant field such as data science, computer science, information systems, business, or a related discipline, e.g. a STEM subject or a finance-related subject.Equivalent professional experience will be considered where a degree is not held.
