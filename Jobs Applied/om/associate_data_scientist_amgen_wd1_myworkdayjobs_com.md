Collect, organize, and analyze data related to product performance (e.g., APRs) and patient complaints. Ensure data integrity, accuracy, and accessibility for further analysis. Utilize statistical software and data visualization tools to interpret and present findings. Identify trends, patterns, and insights from large datasets to inform decision-making processes. Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and company policies in all documentation and reporting. Collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop and implement quality improvement initiatives based on data analysis findings. Foster strong relationships with external and internal partners. Data Cleaning: Ensuring data integrity by identifying and rectifying errors, inconsistencies, and duplications. Database Management: Storing and organizing data in databases or data warehouses for easy access and analysis. Diagnostic Analysis: Investigating the root causes of specific issues, such as recurring product defects or common patient complaints.
