## Job description captured at application time

£85,000 + Benefits
 London (4 days per week in the office)
This is a fantastic opportunity to join a rapidly scaling SaaS business where you can build and lead the Data Engineering function from the ground up whilst remaining hands-on with cutting-edge data and AI initiatives. 

THE COMPANY:

The business works with major enterprise clients across Financial Services, Retail, Telecommunications, Travel and Healthcare. This is an exciting opportunity to join at a pivotal stage of growth and have a genuine impact on the future direction of the business. 

THE ROLE:

You will take ownership of the Data Engineering function, driving technical excellence, engineering best practice and team growth whilst leading the development of scalable, cloud-based data solutions.
Key responsibilities include:
- Building and leading the Data Engineering function, defining standards, processes and ways of working.
- Managing, mentoring and developing a growing team of Data Engineers.
- Designing and delivering scalable ETL/ELT pipelines across operational, analytical and AI data environments.
- Owning data architecture, modelling strategy, CI/CD processes and deployment governance.
- Driving adoption of AI-enabled engineering practices to improve development efficiency, quality and scalability.

YOUR SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

You will bring strong capability in:
- Previous experience leading and line-managing Data Engineering teams.
- Advanced SQL and Python development skills.
- Consultancy experience
- Building and optimising ETL/ELT pipelines within modern cloud environments.
- Data modelling, dimensional modelling and modern data architecture principles.
- CI/CD, Git, DevOps practices and cloud platforms including Azure, AWS and/or GCP.
