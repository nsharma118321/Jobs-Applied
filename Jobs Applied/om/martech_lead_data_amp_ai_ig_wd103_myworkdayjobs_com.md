Your team Our MarTech team is evolving as we take on projects that will reshape how we communicate with clients, personalise their experiences, improve marketing performance and embed AI into how our marketing teams work. As MarTech Lead, you will drive the successful delivery of a complex and evolving marketing technology roadmap spanning customer engagement, data infrastructure, performance marketing measurement and AI enablement. Your role in the Team’s Success We're looking for a hands-on MarTech Lead to take technical ownership of our marketing technology stack, not just manage the roadmap, but get into the data and fix what's broken. You'll lead two flagship projects: building a single source of truth by integrating and reconciling data across 40+ tools (Braze, GA4/BigQuery, Salesforce, and more) and owning our SFMC (Salesforce Marketing Cloud) to Braze migration end-to-end, including data validation and technical sign-off. Day to day, you'll diagnose data quality issues directly and use AI tooling to automate the manual work that used to take a whole team. This is a technical role at heart, strong data engineering fundamentals and real fluency with AI-assisted workflows matter more here than project management polish. What you’ll do Own the SFMC -> Braze Migration Act as the technical owner of the SFMC → Braze migration: own the plan, sequencing, and cutover strategy, not just a workstream within it Personally validate data and journey parity between SFMC and Braze, reconciling records, logic, and event data at the SQL/pipeline level, not just sign-off via a status report Identify and fix migration-related data issues directly (mapping errors, duplicate/missing events, broken triggers) rather than logging tickets for someone else to resolve Own the technical relationship with Client Tech and Data Architecture for everything migration-related, uptime, data integrity, and rollback/contingency planning Build the source of truth data Define the technical architecture for a unique source of truth, schema design, identity resolution/stitching across tools, update cadence, and ownership of conflicts when tools disagree Manage integrations of 3rd-party tools, understanding the technical data contracts involved, spec how data from disparate tools should be integrated, deduplicated, and reconciled into a single, trusted data store. Work with Data Architecture and the data warehouse team to align this with the existing BigQuery/attribution setup Build validation/monitoring so the source of truth is trusted by default, flag drift or breakage before stakeholders notice bad numbers Technical Ownership Diagnose and resolve data quality issues directly. Duplicate events, integer overflow/ID issues, broken field mappings, integration failures rather than escalating to a data engineer Write and maintain SQL/Python for data validation, QA scripts, and user journey debugging.
