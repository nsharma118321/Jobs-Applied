This role is critical in ensuring accuracy, availability and actionable use of data to drive effective commercial decision-making within the Commercial team on all matters relating to third party spend incurred by AWE. Key Accountabilities: Ensures Commercial teams have access to timely, accurate and relevant data to meet performance and strategic objectives. Accountable for master data management of all commercial data, ensuring accuracy, consistency and integrity across systems used by the Commercial function.  Accountable for managing the data lifecycle, ensuring seamless data flow across systems.   Accountable for data protocols, data cleansing and data maintenance to ensure usable data is made available to Commercial colleagues.  Accountable for liaison between Commercial, IT, Finance and other relevant teams in AWE to align data strategy with business requirements.  Accountable for the management of the Commercial Data Team. A natural analytical thinker. Proficient in SQL, Excel and visualisation platforms (e.g. Power BI, Tableau). Strong communication skills with ability to translate technical insights into business language. Detail oriented with a proactive approach to problem-solving and process improvement.
