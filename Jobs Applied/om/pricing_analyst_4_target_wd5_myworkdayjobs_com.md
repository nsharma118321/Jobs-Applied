PRIMARY FUNCTION The Pricing Analyst’s core responsibility is to govern and execute retail price adjustments at the enterprise level to ensure execution matched our intended pricing strategy for each category &amp; item. Analysts own the regular operations and performance metrics. They advance the pricing operations performance for Target by building effective and efficient processes that pair with product technology developments to drive ongoing evolving pricing capabilities. PRINCIPAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES · Be the single point of contact for pricing operations supporting an assigned business (online &amp; stores) and gain expertise on end to end pyramid and strategy context. This includes regular audits and research of pricing at Target and competitors, reviewing outliers in competitive price changes, and new item setups. · Build category context to pair with rigorous data analysis on the drivers of pricing metrics and performance versus enterprise objectives · Drive, lead and deliver new tasks and innovation projects flawlessly with 100 % accuracy and timeliness · Solve defined problems of high complexity and streamline/redefine processes using structured techniques or automation · Drive stakeholder and organizational awareness to performance metrics by creating and publishing dashboards, performing deep dive analyses to provide insight into effectiveness of pricing strategies and operations. · Effectively manage best practice and team governance for your business/process and provide strategic solutions to key areas across pricing.
