Data Scientist (Credit Risk)
Location: Crewe (on-site) Salary: £50k - £60k + 10% Bonus + Benefits

Gravitas are delighted to be exclusively partnering with a market-leading global technology and mobility organisation to recruit a Data Scientist (Credit Risk).

This is a unique opportunity to join a high-growth business that is investing heavily in data and analytics. You'll play a pivotal role in designing and deploying predictive models that sit at the heart of the company's credit decisioning process, directly influencing commercial outcomes and risk strategy.

If you're passionate about turning complex data into actionable insights and enjoy building models that drive real-world decisions, we'd love to hear from you.

The Opportunity
As a Data Scientist within the Credit team, you'll be responsible for the end-to-end development, validation, and monitoring of credit risk models across the customer lifecycle. Working closely with senior stakeholders across Credit, Finance, Commercial, and Analytics, you'll translate business challenges into data science solutions that drive smarter lending and credit decisions.
This is a highly visible role offering significant autonomy, ownership, and the opportunity to shape the future direction of credit modelling within a rapidly evolving business.

Key Responsibilities
- Design, develop, validate, and maintain credit risk models, including:
- Application scorecards
- Behavioural scorecards
- Recovery propensity models
- Customer lifetime value models
- Apply modern machine learning and statistical techniques including gradient boosting, classification, regression, and survival analysis.
- Translate commercial and credit risk challenges into structured modelling problems.
- Build robust monitoring frameworks to track model performance and stability over time.
- Assess model effectiveness using measures such as Gini, CAP, population stability, and other relevant performance metrics.
- Work directly with complex, real-world datasets from multiple source systems to prepare model-ready data.
- Support and contribute to broader data modelling and data engineering activities within the Credit function.
- Present findings and model outputs clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
- Ensure methodologies, assumptions, and outputs are fully documented and governed.

About You
We're looking for a hands-on Data Scientist with a strong quantitative mindset and a genuine interest in credit risk and predictive modelling.

Essential Skills & Experience
- 3+ years' experience in a Data Science, Credit Risk Modelling, Quantitative Analytics, or similar role.
- Proven experience building and validating predictive models in a commercial environment.
- Strong Python and SQL skills.
- Experience working across classification and regression modelling techniques.
- Practical experience handling large, complex datasets from multiple sources.
- Strong stakeholder engagement and communication skills.
- Ability to explain complex modelling concepts and limitations to business audiences.
Desirable Experience
- Credit risk, lending, financial services, banking, fintech, or related domain experience.
- Experience with survival analysis.
- Knowledge of model governance, monitoring, and validation frameworks.
- Experience developing scorecards or decisioning models.
