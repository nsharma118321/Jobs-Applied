Role Summary The Business Intelligence Lead is responsible for designing, delivering, and supporting reporting and analytics solutions for both internal stakeholders and external clients. Working closely with clients, project teams, solution architects, and technical teams, the role leads the end-to-end delivery of reporting workstreams, from requirements gathering and solution design through to implementation, user adoption, and continuous improvement. The role combines strong technical expertise with stakeholder management, consultancy, and leadership skills. Core Duties/Responsibilities The successful candidate will be responsible for the following: Design and deliver reporting and analytics solutions using Power BI, SQL, Azure, and related technologies. Develop dashboards, KPI frameworks, reporting packs, management information solutions, and client-facing analytics products. Lead requirements gathering sessions, stakeholder workshops, and discovery activities to define reporting needs and business objectives. Build strong relationships with clients, stakeholders, and project teams, acting as the reporting subject matter expert throughout project delivery. Present reporting solutions, analytics, and recommendations to senior stakeholders, clients, and project teams. Translate business requirements into scalable reporting, data, and analytics solutions. Design data models, semantic models, reporting architectures, and KPI frameworks to support operational, strategic, and regulatory reporting requirements. Work closely with data architects, engineers, and technical teams to define and deliver robust data solutions. Ensure the quality, accuracy, reconciliation, and governance of reporting outputs. Produce and maintain data dictionaries, KPI catalogues, reporting inventories, solution documentation, and reporting standards. Support the design and delivery of cloud-based reporting solutions using Azure and modern Microsoft data platforms. Lead reporting workstreams across multiple projects and clients simultaneously, ensuring successful delivery against deadlines and business expectations. The duties and responsibilities outlined above are to be regarded as broad areas of responsibility and do not necessarily detail all tasks which the post holder may be required to perform.
