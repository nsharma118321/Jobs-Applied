THE ROLE

As a Sales Ops Analyst, you’ll be a foundational operational partner to our EMEA sales and customer success teams. You’ll keep the go-to-market engine moving—resolving systems issues, delivering the data and insights needed to make decisions, and removing the day-to-day blockers that slow our customer facing teams down. In doing so, you’ll help build the infrastructure, processes, and operating rhythm that allow our EMEA go-to-market motion to scale with speed and precision.

Key ownership areas:

 - Troubleshoot and resolve GTM systems issues for EMEA sales and customer success teams during UK business hours.

 - Handle data requests and reporting for go-to-market stakeholders, including sales and engagement leadership.

 - Establish and manage deal desk processes, oversee deal flow, and give sales reps the operational support they need.

 - Build dashboarding and business intelligence infrastructure using a BI tool rather than relying solely on Salesforce dashboards or manual Google Sheets reporting.

 - Manage Chargebee access and provide billing system support, reducing the need for sales reps to work directly in Chargebee and lowering the risk of errors.

 - Create scalable processes for recurring data requests, systems troubleshooting, and cross-time-zone handoffs.

 - Document and continuously improve go-to-market operations workflows.

Early priorities:

 - Establish documented, repeatable deal desk workflows with clear handoff procedures.

 - Implement dashboarding infrastructure that enables deeper insights and reduces reliance on manual reporting.

 - Create reliable systems for resolving recurring data requests and operational issues.

WHAT YOU BRING

 - Strong sales operations acumen, including familiarity with sales operations, deal desk workflows, revenue systems, and the full sales funnel.

 - The ability to identify operational bottlenecks, diagnose their root causes, and design scalable systems and processes.

 - Technical fluency with business tools such as Salesforce, Chargebee, BI tools such as Hex, and data platforms.

 - Comfort moving between systems, understanding data flows, and implementing operational solutions without heavy engineering support.

 - The ability to translate business questions into useful reporting, dashboards, and clear operational recommendations.

 - High ownership and autonomy, with sound judgment about when to solve independently and when to escalate.

 - A proactive approach to supporting sales and customer success teams, anticipating needs before they become blockers.

 - Clear communication, strong attention to detail, and comfort operating in a fast-moving environment.
