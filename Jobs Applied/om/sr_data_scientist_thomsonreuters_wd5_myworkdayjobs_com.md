Job Description Join the Data &amp; Analytics team supporting Legal AI products. As a Senior Data Scientist, you will leverage Machine Learning, Predictive Analytics, and Generative AI to drive customer acquisition, retention, upsell, and cross-sell opportunities through data-driven insights. Key Responsibilities Build propensity, churn, and classification models. Develop GenAI and LLM-based solutions, including AI agents. Use web scraping and data enrichment techniques to identify new customer opportunities. Analyze customer data to generate actionable business insights. Partner with stakeholders to solve business problems using analytics. Deploy ML models and communicate findings through dashboards and presentations. Required Qualifications 2-4 years of Data Science experience. Strong Python, SQL, and Machine Learning expertise. Experience with Generative AI, LLMs, prompt engineering, and AI agents. Experience with AWS and Amazon SageMaker. Strong analytical, communication, and presentation skills. Bachelor's or Master's degree in a quantitative discipline. Preferred Qualifications Experience with sales and marketing analytics. Knowledge of LegalTech or B2B professional services. Experience with Tableau, Power BI, or similar tools.
