Introducing the Trainline Customer Growth Team 👋

The Customer Growth team helps Trainline understand and improve how our marketing builds awareness, consideration and long-term customer growth. As a Data Scientist specialising in Marketing Measurement, you will shape how we evaluate marketing performance, turning complex and imperfect data into clear recommendations that support high-value commercial decisions.

You will take ownership of a varied portfolio of focused, fast-moving measurement projects, using Python, causal inference and geo-experimentation to understand marketing impact. Working closely with marketing, analytics and other cross-functional partners, you will combine multiple signals where no single measurement approach provides the full answer, helping stakeholders make informed decisions with confidence.

In this role as the Data Scientist, you will... 🚄

- Design and deliver robust approaches for measuring the effectiveness and incremental impact of marketing activity.
- Use Python, causal inference and geo-experimentation techniques to analyse performance and generate commercially relevant insights.
- Combine evidence from multiple data sources and measurement methods to provide a clear and balanced view where no single solution is definitive.
- Translate complex analytical findings into practical recommendations that enable marketing stakeholders to make informed investment decisions.
- Partner closely with marketing, analytics and other cross-functional teams to define measurement questions, shape project scope and align on successful outcomes.
- Manage several focused analytical projects at the same time, balancing pace, quality and business value across competing priorities.
- Apply pragmatic judgement when working with incomplete or imperfect data, clearly communicating assumptions, limitations and levels of confidence.
- Contribute to the continued development of Trainline's marketing measurement capabilities, identifying opportunities to improve methods, tools and ways of working.

We'd love to hear from you if you have... 🔎

- Strong practical experience using Python to analyse data, build measurement approaches and communicate meaningful findings.
- Experience applying causal inference, geo-experimentation or related experimental and quasi-experimental methods to real-world business questions.
- A pragmatic and commercially focused approach, with the ability to balance analytical rigour with the need to make timely decisions.
- Strong communication skills, including the ability to explain technical concepts clearly to stakeholders with different levels of analytical expertise.
- Confidence managing several projects or workstreams simultaneously while maintaining focus on the most valuable outcomes.
- Experience working collaboratively with marketing, analytics or other cross-functional teams to turn business questions into analytical solutions.
- Ideally, experience in marketing measurement, marketing effectiveness or marketing mix modelling, although we also welcome applicants with relevant transferable experience.
- Curiosity, sound judgement and a willingness to work with ambiguity, test different approaches and learn from imperfect evidence.
