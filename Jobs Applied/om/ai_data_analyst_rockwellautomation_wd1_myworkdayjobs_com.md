Job Description Position Summary The AI Data Analyst will be part of the Supply Chain Analytics team and will help design and deploy AI-enabled analytics solutions that improve decision-making, productivity, and business outcomes. This role combines business analysis, data analytics, and applied AI. The successful candidate will work directly with business stakeholders to understand workflows and business problems, identify opportunities for analytics and AI, and translate those needs into practical solutions. The role will use platforms such as Databricks, Copilot Studio, Power Platform, and other enterprise analytics and AI tools to deliver analytics, automation, and decision-support solutions. Key Responsibilities Partner with other teams to understand workflows, pain points, decisions, and opportunities for analytics, automation, and AI. Identify AI and analytics use cases that measurable business value. Translate requirements into AI-enabled analytics, decision-support tools, and analytical applications. Apply generative AI, machine learning, predictive analytics, and other AI techniques to improve decisions and workflows. Define requirements, process flows, success metrics, and adoption plans for analytics and AI use cases. Test AI-enabled solutions for accuracy, usefulness, reliability, and business fit. Develop business cases and value estimates for AI and analytics solutions. Develop expertise in supply-chain systems, business processes, data sources, and end-to-end data flows, and use that knowledge to connect business problems with the right data and analytical approach. Collaborate with Data Engineering, AI Engineering, and business teams to develop, deploy, and support scalable solutions. Support user adoption, training, governance, and continuous improvement of deployed solutions. The Essentials - You Will Have: Bachelor's degree in Analytics, Information Systems, Computer Science, Engineering, Supply Chain, Business, or a related field. Strong SQL, Python, and analytical problem-solving skills. Hands-on experience using Databricks and working with enterprise data sources, including SAP or comparable ERP systems. Experience gathering requirements, documenting workflows, and translating business needs into analytical or technical solutions. Experience developing dashboards, decision-support tools, analytical applications, or other data-driven business solutions. Experience applying analytics, AI/ML, or statistical techniques to solve business problems and support decision-making. Understanding of data quality, governance, and master data management principles. The Preferred - You Might Also Have: Experience with Azure AI Foundry, Copilot Studio, Power Platform, or similar AI platforms. 5+ years of professional experience, including 3+ years in data analytics, business analytics, AI-enabled analytics, or analytics solution development. Experience with generative AI, AI agents, prompt engineering, or Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Experience applying machine learning, predictive analytics, or decision intelligence to business problems. Experience with GitHub and source-control practices. Experience developing or supporting modern analytical applications using React, TypeScript, REST APIs, or similar technologies. Familiarity with Azure cloud services and deployment of business applications. Experience working in Supply Chain, Procurement, Sourcing, Planning, Manufacturing, Logistics, Quality, or Operations environments. Understanding of AI governance, security, and responsible AI practices.
